Terminology policy (book 2)

Written 2026-10-05 (Stage 1b). Applies to every chapter, exercise README, appendix and Obsidian note. It extends the rules in CONVENTIONS.md ("Hebrew first, English in parentheses on first use") with a domain decision: which words stay English and which are translated, and the one rendering each translated concept uses. Where book 1 (ai-engineering-course, appendix A) already has a rendering, this book reuses it unless the brief fixed another.

1. Rule of thumb

  • Product nouns and their own vocabulary stay English, with a Hebrew gloss on first use in a chapter: "pod (היחידה הקטנה ביותר שרצה ב־Kubernetes: קונטיינר אחד או יותר)". Afterwards the English word, unformatted in prose when it is a concept (pod, topic), in backticks when it is an identifier or a command (kubectl, Deployment as a manifest kind).
  • Concepts are translated, one rendering each, listed below. On first use per chapter: Hebrew, then English in parentheses. Never alternate between two Hebrew renderings of the same concept in one book.
  • Metrics and acronyms stay English as in book 1: p99, RED, USE, SLI, MTTR, QPS, ACID, CAP, PACELC, RPO/RTO. SLO is introduced once as "יעד רמת שירות (SLO)" and then written SLO.
  • Hebrew grammar attaches with a maqaf: ה־pod, ב־Kafka, ל־goroutine. Plurals of English nouns: "שלושה pods", not "פודים"; in prose about a kind of object prefer the English plural.
  • A new term not in this file: pick one rendering, use it consistently, add it to the chapter's terms table (appendix A is generated from those tables) and, if it will recur across chapters, add it here in the same change.

4. Words to avoid

Avoid Use
„פודים”, „קונטיינרים” as Hebrew plurals in technical prose pods, containers
„ריטריי”, „טיימאאוט”, „קאש” ניסיון חוזר, זמן קצוב, מטמון
„דיפלוי” פריסה (deploy as a verb: לפרוס)
„בק־אנד” in Hebrew letters backend
„מימוש” for deployment פריסה
„לרנדר” להפיק (a template), להציג (a page)
